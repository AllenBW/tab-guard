Welcome to Tab Guard

Tab Guard watches your browser's tabs from the menu bar (Firefox and Chrome, and browsers built on them). When a tab grows past your limits it can close it, and the browser shows that tab as crashed, with a button to reload it. New installs start in Watch only, so nothing is closed until you pick another mode above.

The first time you use Which site is which? with Firefox, macOS asks whether Tab Guard may control it. That's only used to open its about:processes page.
