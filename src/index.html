Welcome to Tab Guard

Tab Guard watches your browsers' tab processes from the menu bar (Firefox, Chrome, and browsers built on them). When one grows past your limits it can close it. The browser then shows that process's tabs as crashed, with a button to reload them, and anything unsaved in them is lost.

New installs start in Watch only: nothing is closed until you pick another mode at the top of the window. The limits are 2 GB per tab process and 8 GB per browser; change them under Limits & settings. To stop it, use Pause in the menu bar or the switch at the top right.

Tab Guard starts when you log in and installs its updates itself. You can turn either off in Limits & settings.

Memory is each process's own footprint (what Activity Monitor shows). One tab process can hold several tabs. Browsers don't tell other apps which website runs in which process; Firefox's about:processes and Chrome's process-internals pages do.
